This is a script that will highlight features of Google Cloud's Chirp 3 HD Voice generation.

If your text file is authored with a professional style of sentence structure and formatting, the resulting speech will sound more professional. ... At the same time, this may sound a bit more robotic...

However, if you'll change your sentence formatting to something that's a bit more similar to something that you'd use in a text - it becomes a little more relaxed and natural...

Here's an example demonstrating this. We'll start off with something a little more rigid and robotic...

Reservation confirmation.
Name is Emily Carter.
Date is August 2nd.
Time is 7:30 PM.
Number of guests is 4.
Restaurant is The Oak Bistro.
Table number is 16.
Special note: One guest has a food allergy.
Please arrive 5 minutes early.
Call 855-555-9832 for changes.

Now, here is the more natural sounding version...

Hi Emily! Just confirming your reservation at The Oak Bistro—you're all set for 4 guests on August 2nd at 7:30 PM.

You’ll be at table 16, and we’ve noted that someone in your party has a food allergy—thanks for letting us know!

If anything changes, feel free to call us at 855-555-9832. Looking forward to having you!
